Food & Dishes

Everyday dishes, cravings, and how to say something tastes good
About this lesson
Cantonese meals are built on a small set of workhorse words. 飯 (faan6) anchors most meals, while 粥 (zuk1) is the lighter everyday option. Pair either with 好味 (hou2 mei6) to say it tastes good.

What changed from v1

  • Nothing is filled with brand colour any more. Tier pills, subtabs and the revealed word card were all solid orange blocks; they're now outline, underline, and a pale tint respectively. Orange survives as text, borders and the 2px accent rule.
  • Shadows are effectively gone. The two elevation tiers are replaced by a hairline border plus a whisper shadow. Surfaces are separated by line and space, not by lift.
  • Sentences and tones became divided lists, not stacks of cards. Fewer boxes competing for attention — same .card primitive, wrapping rows instead of repeating per item.
  • Spacing scale extended (added 28/36/48px steps) and applied — section gaps roughly doubled.
  • Emoji dropped from the title and section headers. Titles now sit in Fraunces at 30px with a letterspaced eyebrow above, which is where the editorial quality in your reference actually comes from.
  • English reveal set in the display serif rather than UI sans — makes the answer feel like the considered payoff of the card.

Still open

  • The six tone colours remain untouched and are now the loudest thing on screen. They're functional (consistent app-wide), so changing them is a separate decision — but worth making.
  • Header treatment still toggleable above; no decision assumed.
