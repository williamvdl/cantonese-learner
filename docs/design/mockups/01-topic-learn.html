🍜 Food & Dishes

Everyday dishes, cravings, and how to say something's delicious
📌 About this lesson
Cantonese meals are built around a small set of workhorse words — 飯 (faan6) "rice" anchors most meals, while 粥 (zuk1) "congee" is the classic light option. Pair any dish with 好味 (hou2 mei6) to say it's delicious.

What's different from the live app

  • One .card primitive for every surface (note, word, sentence, tone guide) instead of 10 independently defined card classes with drifting radius/border/shadow.
  • One radius scale (8 / 12 / 18 / pill) replacing the ~15 hardcoded values currently scattered through the CSS.
  • Per-topic accent colours (the old lesson.color per category) dropped in favour of the single brand accent — matches the "Category accent colours switched off" decision already made but not yet fully carried through.
  • The header is toggleable above so you can compare both directions live rather than judging them from swatches.
  • Signature element: the oversized low-opacity character in the lesson band uses the actual word being studied, not a generic decorative shape — ties the visual identity directly to the content.
