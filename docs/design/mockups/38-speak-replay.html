A play button while speaking

Two speaking surfaces have no way to hear the line: the checkpoint's sentence review after an attempt, and the conversation's your turn step. The Say it back sheet on topic sentences already has one, in its header beside the close button. This page draws three places to put it, each on both surfaces, using the real v153 card.

Behaviour is the same whichever placement you choose. It plays the line with the audio the rest of the app uses, shows the speaker icon while playing like the sheet does, and is disabled while the mic is listening, since played-back audio would feed straight into the recogniser as a trivially correct match. In the checkpoint it appears only once the sentence is on screen, after an attempt or Show me the answer. DES-44 kept audio out of Say it in Cantonese before an attempt because hearing it first hands over the answer, and this keeps that intact. The conversation shows the line before you speak, so there it's available from the start, like the sheet.

The checkpoint frames are in the checkpoint's oxblood (--milestone) and the conversation frames in the path's orange, as each screen is today.

MOCK-38-corner · in the card's top-right corner recommended

One position on every speaking surface: top right, where the Say it back sheet already keeps its play button in the header. It is there before an attempt and after one, never competes with the mic, and adds nothing to the action row. It gets a slim top row of its own, shared with the conversation's You label, because a first draft floated it over the card and a long line such as 我想買蘋果。有幾多個？ (ngo5 soeng2 maai5 ping4 gwo2. jau5 gei2 do1 go3?) ran underneath it. On the checkpoint card, which has no label, that row costs about 36px of height.

MOCK-38-pair · beside the mic

Listen and speak side by side, the pairing Translate uses (DES-49). It reads well before an attempt, when the mic is the focus. After an attempt the pair is stranded mid-card, above the result the learner is now reading, so the button is furthest from where the eye is. The mic stays centred, with an empty slot on its right to balance the play button.

Beside Try again, where the eye lands after a miss. But it only exists once there is a result, so it can't serve the conversation before an attempt, which you asked for.
