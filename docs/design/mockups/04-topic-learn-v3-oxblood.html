Food & Dishes

Everyday dishes, cravings, and how to say something tastes good
About this lesson
Cantonese meals are built on a small set of workhorse words. 飯 (faan6) anchors most meals, while 粥 (zuk1) is the lighter everyday option. Pair either with 好味 (hou2 mei6) to say it tastes good.
