Part 2 · Tone colours

Current

我好餓，想食點心同餃子。
I'm hungry — I want dim sum and dumplings.

Option 1 — harmonised spectrum. Same six hue positions, so nothing you've learned is invalidated. Normalised to one lightness band and lower chroma: no dot outshouts another, all six clear WCAG AA on white, and none sits in the brand-orange lane.

我好餓，想食點心同餃子。
I'm hungry — I want dim sum and dumplings.

Option 2 — contour families. Hue encodes the pitch contour — blue for level tones, green for rising, warm for falling — and lightness encodes pitch height. The colour teaches the system instead of just labelling it. The cost: six-way at-a-glance discrimination gets weaker, since tones inside a family look related by design.
