Topic top area, and type on the learning path

Mocked against v120. Two unrelated problems that happen to sit next to each other in the same journey: the stack of chrome above a lesson, and the fact that two different serifs are doing the job of one across the path screens. The nameplate reads 廣東話 (gwong2 dung1 waa2).

What is measured, not assumed. Every "now" frame below is transcribed from styles.css at v120 rather than drawn from memory. The type finding is a count of var(--font-serif) call sites: 10 in total, of which 2 are correct (.wm, the watermark, and .cp-missed-c, which is Chinese) and 8 are Latin titles or numerals that DESIGN_SYSTEM §1 assigns to --font-display.

1 · The context block, arriving from a path

Three elements stack under the header with nothing to tell them apart: the context row, a 2px progress track, and the stage stepper. All three sit on --parchment-warm, which is also the page background — so the block has no edges at all, and the only visible boundary in the whole region is the track. When a stage is fully complete the track fills to 100% and becomes a full-bleed orange rule through the middle of the screen, which is what it is doing on First Words in the device screenshot. Mockup 10 gave the lesson band a border-bottom; nothing in the built version has one.

3 · Two serifs are doing the job of one

DESIGN_SYSTEM §1 is explicit: --font-display (Fraunces) is for "page and section titles, score values, English reveals"; --font-serif (Noto Serif TC) is for "all Chinese text, the nameplate, watermarks". Noto Serif TC ships full Latin coverage and is loaded with it, so every Latin title set in --font-serif silently renders in the Chinese face rather than falling through to Fraunces. That is why "Learning Path" and "Beginner" do not look like "Greetings" — and the two are one tap apart.

Weight moves with the face. All eight sites are --fw-bold (700). Fraunces at 700 is heavier than Noto Serif TC at 700 and would out-shout .lesson-title, which sits at --fw-semi. Moving the face without moving the weight would trade one mismatch for another, so the proposal is --font-display at --fw-semi throughout — matching the one title that is already right.

4 · The learning path landing

DESIGN_SYSTEM §3.6: emoji are "not in titles, section headers, result screens, or path step rows", kept only where they are genuine wayfinding — the Topics category grid, where one icon per topic aids scanning a large set. The path landing has emoji in the page title (🛤️) and in each card title (🌱 🌿 🌳), and the path timeline header repeats the card's icon. None of these is one-icon-per-item wayfinding: there are three cards and the level is already stated in the section header directly above, so the sprout is decorating a word that is already there.

5 · Also found, not proposed here

41 selectors carry the same property in two separate rules. Standing check 1 only inspects a single rule, so it cannot see this: .path-card-title declares font-size:16px at one place and --fs-lead (17px) at another, and the later one silently wins. The same holds for .lesson-title, .path-timeline-title, .quiz-chinese and 37 more. Nothing is visibly broken — the later declaration is generally the intended one — but every dead declaration is a place where a future edit lands with no effect. This is the same failure shape STATUS.md records for .cp-done .cp-card .path-step-title at v108. It wants a script and a sweep of its own, not a fix bolted onto this work.
