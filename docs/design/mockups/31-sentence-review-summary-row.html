MOCK-31 — what a sentence-review summary row shows

The list after a checkpoint sentence run. Today it is Chinese only. Three options for what each row carries, using the sentence classes the Learn screen already uses. The tally, the badges and the buttons are unchanged in all three.

MOCK-31-A
Chinese and jyutping

The row you can read aloud. Two lines. Keeps the list short enough that eight rows still fit roughly one screen, and gives the pronunciation back for anything that went wrong. Says nothing about meaning — if you have forgotten what a sentence meant, this will not tell you.

Chinese, jyutping and English

Everything, always. Three lines per row; eight rows becomes a scroll rather than a glance. Nothing is hidden and nothing needs a tap, which matters because the testing has already happened by the time this screen appears — there is no recall left to protect.
