## Reading the result

Written before any marks exist, so the reading cannot bend to fit them.

- **A marker stays on the register list** if at least half of its lines are marked *Off* or *I'd say…*, **and** that is a clearly higher share than the controls get.
- **A marker comes off the list** if most of its lines are marked *Fine*.
- **你好嗎？ (nei5 hou2 maa3?)**, lines 12 and 20, is read separately from the other six 嗎 (maa3) lines — it is a set greeting and may be fine where the others are not.
- **The control lines are the baseline.** If several controls are marked *Off*, the finding is no longer about the three markers: it says the corpus has more unnatural lines than the scan can see, and a wider sample is the next step.
- **14 lines per group is small.** One or two marks either way is noise; this can show a clear difference, not a fine one.
- **Every *Off* and *I'd say…* line is logged** with its before and after — these are the first entries in the known-bad set.
