Tone Reference Check

Builds canonical tone contours from the existing TTS audio · build R2 · read-only

Reads the corpus, finds every single-syllable word with a clean tone digit, and analyses its existing clip. Nothing is recorded and nothing is written.
Canonical contours
Semitones relative to the TTS voice's own median, across the syllable. These are the target shapes a learner take would be graded against.
