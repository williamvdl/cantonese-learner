A · Corpus sweep no recording needed

Every multi-syllable word with clean tone digits, analysed from its existing clip. Predicts the number of voiced runs from the jyutping, measures how many there actually are, then grades each recovered syllable.

The prediction is fixed by spelling and no setting changes it. The bar, stated before the run: LONG above SHORT, SHORT above NONE. If no setting produces that ordering, the model is wrong rather than the settings. Run all 7 works through a grid fixed in the source — one setting moved at a time from the baseline — and appends every run to a single log, so the whole set can be copied once. The three dropdowns apply to the single-run button only; the grid ignores them and sets its own.

B · Your own voice the part the sweep can't answer

A synthesiser articulates consonants more crisply than a person does. These nine words are drawn from the corpus, three per class, so the same prediction can be tested on real speech. Say each word once, at the pace you would say it to someone — not slowly and not separated into syllables, because separating them is what creates the break we are trying to find out whether you produce.
