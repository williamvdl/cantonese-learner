ASR Recognition Probe —

Does a wrong tone produce a different decoded word? Run 1 is calibration — it can conclude stop or continue, not build.

Tap Record, wait a beat, then speak. Bare syllables are very short — hold the vowel a little rather than clipping it. Recording stops itself after 6s.

Recogniser settings

These are properties of the recogniser and are stored with each attempt. Changing them mid-run makes attempts non-comparable — the summary flags it if you do. Mode is a dial rather than a fixed choice because which one works is exactly what is in doubt — run the same syllable through both before concluding anything about the recogniser.

The recogniser returns characters only. The jyutping and English beside them are looked up from the corpus — they are not something the recogniser reported, and they say nothing about which tone it heard.

Discard freely. A discarded attempt costs one retake; a kept bad one silently corrupts the result and cannot be untangled afterwards.

Match policy — applied at analysis time

Recordings are stored with their full N-best list, so every policy can be scored against the same audio. Tightening policy raises detection and false rejects together.
