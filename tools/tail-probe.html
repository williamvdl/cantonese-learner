Tail probe — is the final syllable being truncated?

One question only: when the app loses the last syllable of a sentence, is it because the recogniser never heard it, or because we stop listening too early and throw away an interim result that had it?

What this is testing

Background. 一加二係三 (jat1 gaa1 ji6 hai6 saam1) came back from the app as 一家以孩 — the first three syllables acoustically correct, saam1 missing entirely. Either failure alone would still have passed the matcher; it was the two together that failed it.

Two candidate causes, and this tells them apart. (1) Stop timing — finishListening() calls stop() the instant you tap, and the tail may still be in flight. (2) Discarded interims — the app matches on the FINAL transcript only, so if an interim contained the last syllable and the final pass dropped it, we already had the answer and threw it away.

Each attempt below records every interim and every final with timestamps, and reports separately whether the last syllable appeared in any interim versus in the final. Run the same sentence at each stop delay and compare.

Target

Do all four, several times each. The 0 ms and 6000 ms rows are the ones that matter most: if the last syllable survives at 6000 ms but not at 0 ms, stop timing is the cause and the fix is small.
